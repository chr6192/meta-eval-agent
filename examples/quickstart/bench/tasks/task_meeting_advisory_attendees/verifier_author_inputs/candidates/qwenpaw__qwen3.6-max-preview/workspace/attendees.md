**Date:** Wednesday, May 30, 2012
**Location:** Room 4830, Herbert C. Hoover Building, 1401 Constitution Avenue, N.W., Washington, D.C.
**Time:** 10:00 a.m. – 11:27 a.m.

| **Jennifer Warren** | Vice President, Technology Policy & Regulation, Lockheed Martin Corporation | Asked substantive questions about spreading company engineering expertise across multiple working groups; raised concerns about the need for a common industry input/baseline across all five working groups before they begin |
