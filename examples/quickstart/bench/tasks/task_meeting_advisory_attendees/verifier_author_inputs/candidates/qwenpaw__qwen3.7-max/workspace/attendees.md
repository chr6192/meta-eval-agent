**Date:** May 30, 2012
**Location:** Room 4830, Herbert C. Hoover Building, 1401 Constitution Avenue, N.W., Washington, D.C.
**Presiding:** Dr. Brian Fontes, Chair

| 9 | **Carl Povelites** | Assistant Vice President of Public Policy, AT&T | In-person | **Substantive.** Asked whether cost estimates from the 1755–1850 report would be revisited; suggested the existing "Search for 500 MHz" subcommittee technical characteristics could serve as a baseline for the new working groups. |
| 10 | **Dr. Charles Rush** | CMR Consulting | In-person | **Substantive.** Asked multiple detailed questions: (1) who would perform sharing analyses; (2) how uniformity of commercial deployment assumptions would be ensured across the four working groups operating in the same frequency range; (3) drew on FCC/ITU-R precedent (IMT-2000) to advocate for agreed-upon parameters before working groups begin; offered to help on that effort. |
| 11 | **Bryan Tramont, Esq.** | Managing Partner, Wilkinson Barker Knauer, LLP | In-person | **Substantive.** Clarified the meeting timeline and working group reporting schedule; proposed that working group co-chairs report back within ten days on what to wrap up by July; argued that consistent cross-group industry representation is more achievable than a separate pre-coordination step. |
| 12 | **Jennifer Warren** | Vice President, Technology Policy & Regulation, Lockheed Martin Corporation | In-person | **Substantive.** Asked whether engineers with different technology specializations could be distributed across the five working groups; pressed for a common set of commercial input parameters to be established before working groups begin, to avoid inconsistent outcomes. |
